Food Descriptions

Pizza:
A delicious Italian dish made with dough, tomato sauce, cheese, and various toppings.
Burgers:
A sandwich consisting of a cooked patty of ground meat, usually beef, placed inside a sliced bun.
Sushi:
A Japanese dish consisting of vinegared rice accompanied by various ingredients such as seafood, vegetables, and occasionally tropical fruits.
Salads:
A cold dish consisting of a mixture of raw or cooked vegetables, often served with a dressing.
Dosa:
A thin, crispy pancake made from fermented rice and lentil batter, commonly served with chutney and sambar in South Indian cuisine.
Idly:
A soft, spongy cake made from fermented rice and lentil batter, typically served with sambar and coconut chutney.
Poori:
A deep-fried bread made from maida (refined wheat flour), often served with dal and vegetables.
Chapati:
A whole wheat flatbread that is a staple in Indian cuisine, typically eaten with various curries.
Parotta:
A layered flatbread that is commonly consumed in South India, often served with curry or stew.
